What happens to selected text

Nothing is sent when you hover or select. Only after you choose Fallacies, Facts, or Bias, the selected passage is sent securely to Jiddu, processed by model providers, and stored in your account analysis history. The page title and URL stay in this Chrome session and are not sent to Jiddu.

Jiddu does not sell this data or use it for advertising.
Jiddu can be wrong. Review the findings and sources.jiddu.app
